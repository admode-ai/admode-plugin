# Admode

Admode builds ad prompts on long-running Meta ads and checks every prompt before anything is rendered: static image ads in 50 formats, UGC video ads in 8 formats, podcast ads, and Pixar-style, claymation and skeleton animated ads. It also writes ad copy, makes new versions of a winning static ad, and makes and keeps your VOC research, Foundation Pack and Brand DNA.

This plugin connects your AI app to Admode's MCP server at `https://mcp.admode.ai/mcp` and adds one skill, `admode`, that tells your AI how to start a job. Admode's tools carry the instructions for each job and run Admode's checks.

Admode writes and checks prompts. It doesn't generate images or video: your AI renders with your own tools, only after you agree.

## What you need

- An Admode plan. It comes with a Skool membership: see [admode.ai/membership](https://admode.ai/membership).
- An AI app that supports plugins or connectors, such as Claude, ChatGPT, Codex, Cursor or VS Code.

When you first use Admode, your app asks you to sign in. Use the email of your Admode plan.

## Privacy

The plugin runs no code on your computer. It sends nothing anywhere except to Admode's own MCP server, and only what your AI passes to Admode's tools: ad prompts, copy, research sections and the details a tool asks for. Admode never sees your chats. Prompts sent for checking aren't stored; your brand, research and anything your AI saves are kept in your Admode account, where you can download or delete them.

Read the [privacy policy](https://admode.ai/privacy) and the [terms of service](https://admode.ai/terms).
