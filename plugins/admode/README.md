# Admode

Your AI turns proven Meta winners into ads for your brand. Admode makes static, AI UGC, podcast and animated ad prompts built on Meta ads that ran for months, written in your customers' words and checked by Admode before you render. It also writes ad copy, makes new versions of a winning static ad, edits a finished video ad, saves your Meta ads and their numbers so new ads build on your winners, and makes and keeps your VOC research, Foundation Pack and Brand DNA.

This plugin connects your AI app to Admode's MCP server at `https://mcp.admode.ai/mcp` and adds one skill, `admode`, that tells your AI how to start each job. Admode's tools carry the instructions for each job and run Admode's checks.

Admode writes and checks prompts. It doesn't generate images or video: your AI renders with your own tools, only after you agree.

## What you need

- An Admode account. The first time you use Admode, your app asks you to sign in: use the email of your Admode account.
- An AI app that supports plugins or connectors, such as Claude, ChatGPT, Codex, Cursor or VS Code.

## Privacy

The plugin runs no code on your computer. It sends nothing anywhere except to Admode's own MCP server, and only what your AI passes to Admode's tools: ad prompts, copy, research sections, the files you upload, the Meta ads data your AI saves and the details a tool asks for. Admode never sees your chats. Prompts sent for checking aren't stored; your brand, research, files and anything your AI saves are kept in your Admode account, where you can download or delete them.

Read the [privacy policy](https://admode.ai/privacy) and the [terms of service](https://admode.ai/terms).
